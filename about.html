Who We Are

NORTHSTAR INTERNATIONAL LLC is a leading Buyer / Seller and exporter of high-quality used CNC machines from the United States. We specialize in sourcing and supplying reliable pre-owned CNC equipment to parts manufacturers, engineering companies, automotive parts makers, aerospace workshops, die-mold makers, and metalworking industries worldwide.

Our inventory regularly includes:

Vertical Machining Centers
Horizontal Machining Centers
CNC Turning Centers & Lathes
Milling Machines
Grinding Machines
CNC Boring Machines
Sheet Metal & Fabrication

We offer machines from renowned brands such as: Mazak, Haas, Hurco, Fadal, Okuma, Mori Seiki, Makino, Doosan, Hyundai, Fanuc, Mitsubishi, Kitamura, Brother, Takisawa, YCM Supermax, Feeler, Hardinge, Bridgeport, Excel and many others.

Our customers across Asia, Africa, the Middle East, and Europe benefit from significant cost savings while obtaining reliable machines that increase production capacity and profitability. We can supply your desired machines to your desired country and across the US with all proper packing, logistics, container loading, export documentation and other custom clearance formalities.
