Who We Are

Leading Buyer / Seller of Used CNC Machines

NORTHSTAR INTERNATIONAL LLC is a leading Buyer / Seller and exporter of high-quality used CNC machines from the United States. We specialize in sourcing and supplying reliable pre-owned CNC equipment to parts manufacturers, engineering companies, automotive parts makers, aerospace workshops, die-mold makers, and metalworking industries worldwide.

We offer machines from renowned brands such as: Mazak, Haas, Hurco, Fadal, Okuma, Mori Seiki, Makino, Doosan, Hyundai, Fanuc, Mitsubishi, Kitamura, Brother, Takisawa, YCM Supermax, Feeler, Hardinge, Bridgeport, Excel and many others.

Full Service Pipeline

Our End-to-End Solutions Overview

From initial machine selection in the United States to final delivery and customs clearance at your international facility, we handle the entire process.

1

1. Sourcing & Acquisition

We leverage our vast network of US manufacturers, aerospace companies, and automotive facilities to source premium, well-maintained used CNC machinery directly from operational plant floors.

2

2. Technical Inspection

Our experienced engineers perform geometry alignment checks, spindle runout tests, backlash calibration, and controller diagnostics. We record full operational videos under power for absolute transparency.

3

3. Custom Machinery Matching

If you require a specific machine model, spindle speed, control system (Fanuc, Mazatrol, Haas), or travel envelope, our dedicated team will scour the United States market to source and match your requirements.

4

4. Rigging & Dismantling

We coordinate licensed and insured heavy-duty rigging crews to dismantle, lift, and remove machinery from U.S. factory spaces, preventing any damage to high-precision axes and linear guides.

5

5. Secure Export Packaging

Machines are wrapped in protective heavy plastic or vacuum-sealed with rust inhibitors, and securely mounted onto custom heavy-duty wood skids. We organize container blocking, bracing, and steel strapping for sea voyages.

6

6. Global Freight & Compliance

Our compliance team handles US Customs clearance, drafts international Bills of Lading, manages documentation, and arranges container transit to major ports across Asia, Africa, Europe, and the Middle East.
